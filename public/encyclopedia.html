2. Authentication: Login & Forced Password Change

2.1 Login Page (/login)

Purpose: Secure single-entry portal for all company personnel. Eliminates demo buttons to enforce strict production credential validation.

  • Email or Employee ID Field:
    • Accepts either your corporate email (vishnu@trash2treasure.com) OR your sequential Employee ID (T2T-001).
  • Password Field:
    • Masked input with a reveal toggle eye icon to check typed characters.
  • "Sign In to Workspace" Button:
    • Sends a secure POST /api/auth/login request.
    • Verifies credentials against Bcrypt hashes in the database.
    • Issues an encrypted HttpOnly JWT cookie (t2t_session_token).
    • Automatically redirects Super Admin/Executives to /dashboard and Employees/Interns to /my-work.

2.2 Forced Password Change (/change-password)

Purpose: Protects newly provisioned accounts so only the employee knows their permanent password.

  • Trigger: If mustChangePassword === true (automatically set when an Admin creates a new user), the system blocks access to all dashboard pages until this form is completed.
  • New Password & Confirm Password Fields:
    • Validates that the password meets security requirements (min 8 characters) and matches.
  • "Activate Account & Save Password" Button:
    • Hashes the new password, updates the user record, sets mustChangePassword = false, and immediately logs the user into their workspace.

3. Page 1: Executive Dashboard (/dashboard)

Purpose: Executive mission-control for Super Admin (CEO), COO, and Department Heads. Aggregates company performance, active sprint momentum, blocked items, and urgent risks in real-time.

+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|  [Total Team: 11]    [Active Projects: 3]    [In Progress: 6]    [Overdue/Blocked: 2] |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|  [ACTIVE SPRINT HEALTH: Sprint 3 (68%)]        |  [DYNAMIC HEALTH SCORE: 85/100]  |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|  [IMMEDIATE ACTION CENTER: Flagged Blockers]   |  [LIVE AUDIT TRAIL: Who & What]  |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+

Every Card & Section Explained

  1. Top KPI Summary Cards (4 Cards):

    • Total Team Members: Live headcount. Clicking takes you to /users.
    • Active Projects: Total projects with status ACTIVE. Clicking takes you to /projects.
    • Tasks In Progress: Total workload currently being executed across all sprints. Clicking takes you to /kanban.
    • Overdue & Blocked: Tasks past their deadline or flagged with an obstacle. Rendered in rose/red to alert leadership. Clicking filters /tasks for urgent resolution.

  2. Active Sprint Health Widget:

    • Committed Points: Story points promised for the current 2-week cycle (e.g. 45 pts).
    • Completed Points: Points delivered so far (e.g. 31 pts).
    • Remaining Days: Countdown to sprint review.
    • Progress Bar: Visual execution velocity percentage.

  3. Dynamic Organization Health Score (0 - 100):

    • Algorithm:
      • Base score = 100.
      • Deducts points for open blockers (5 pts per blocker).
      • Deducts points for overdue tasks (3 pts per overdue task).
      • Adds points proportionally for completed sprint velocity.
    • Status Badges: EXCELLENT (85-100), HEALTHY (70-84), NEEDS ATTENTION (<70).

  4. Immediate Action Center (Urgent Blocker Escalations):

    • Lists tasks where an employee clicked "Flag Blocker".
    • Shows: Task ID, Task Title, Assignee, and the Blocker Reason (e.g., "Waiting for vendor IoT firmware").
    • "Resolve / Inspect" Button: Opens the task to contact the assignee, reassign the task, or log an unblock note.

  5. Live Organizational Audit Feed:

    • Real-time stream of what is happening inside T2T right now (e.g., "Aarav Patel moved T2T-1004 to IN_REVIEW").

4. Page 2: 3-Stage Work Overview (/work-overview)

Purpose: Answers the three most fundamental stakeholder questions without digging through spreadsheets:

  1. What did we complete? (Past)
  2. What are we working on right now? (Current)
  3. What are we building next? (Future)

The 3 Columns Explained

+---------------------------+---------------------------+---------------------------+
|      1. PAST WORK         |     2. CURRENT WORK       |      3. FUTURE WORK       |
| (Completed Deliverables)  |    (In-Flight Execution)  |    (Roadmap & Next Up)    |
+---------------------------+---------------------------+---------------------------+

  1. Past Work (Completed Deliverables):

    • Lists tasks with status COMPLETED.
    • Displays: Task code, title, project name, delivered story points, and completion date.
    • "View Milestone" Button: Shows which project milestone this deliverable unlocked.

  2. Current Work (Active Initiatives):

    • Lists all tasks in IN_PROGRESS or IN_REVIEW.
    • Shows: Assignee avatar, priority pill (URGENT, HIGH), and days remaining until due date.

  3. Future Work (Strategic Roadmap & Upcoming):

    • Lists tasks in TODO or BACKLOG scheduled for upcoming sprint cycles.
    • Shows: Planned story points, target start date, and required skills.

5. Page 3: Personal Workspace — "My Work" (/my-work)

Purpose: The daily home page for Employees, Developers, and Interns. Filters out company-wide noise to focus strictly on: "What must I deliver today?" and "What have I accomplished?"

Header Banner

  • Shows the employee's Monogram Avatar, Full Name, Designation, Department, and Employee ID (e.g., T2T-007).

Interactive Quick Stat Counters (Clickable)

  • Completed Badge (Green): Shows total tasks you've finished. Clicking it switches directly to the "Work History & Delivered" tab.
  • In Progress Badge (Sky Blue): Shows tasks you are actively working on. Clicking it switches to the "Active Workspace" tab.
  • To Do Badge (Amber): Shows tasks waiting in your personal queue.

Overdue Alert Banner (Conditional)

  • Appears only if you have tasks whose dueDate < today.
  • Warns you to either finish the task, update the deadline, or flag a blocker.

The Two Tabs Explained

Tab A: "Active Workspace"

Shows 3 organized task lists:

  1. Currently In Progress:

    • Tasks you are currently developing or executing.
    • Button 1: "Flag Blocker" (Rose Button):
      • Opens the Blocker Dialog. Enter the reason you are stuck (e.g., "Need AWS S3 upload bucket permissions").
      • Clicking "Confirm Blocker" moves the task to BLOCKED, turns it red, and alerts Super Admin on the Executive Dashboard.
    • Button 2: "Submit Review" (Purple Button):
      • Moves task status to IN_REVIEW. Notifies tech leads or managers that work is ready for code review or QA verification.
    • Button 3: "Complete" (Emerald Button with Checkmark):
      • Instantly marks the task COMPLETED. Updates sprint burn-down and moves the card to your Work History.

  2. Tasks Blocked (If Any):

    • Shows any tasks currently paused due to dependencies.
    • Shows the blocker reason clearly in a tinted box.
    • Button: "Unblock & Resume Work" (Green Button):
      • Clears the blocker text, resets status back to IN_PROGRESS, and resumes active tracking.

  3. Next In Queue (To Do):

    • Tasks assigned to you that haven't been started yet.
    • Shows: Task ID, Title, Project, Story points, and Due Date.
    • Button: "Start Working" (Green Play Icon):
      • Moves the task from TODO to IN_PROGRESS with one click. Automatically sets the current start timestamp.

Tab B: "Work History & Delivered Work"

Your permanent record of delivered value at T2T:

  1. Metrics Header:
    • Delivered Work: Total completed tasks count.
    • Story Points Shipped: Total points delivered (e.g. 24 pts).
    • Effort Contributed: Total logged hours (e.g. 42 hrs).
  2. Search Bar: Instant real-time search across your completed deliverables by task title or task ID.
  3. Project Filter Dropdown: Filter your history by a specific project (e.g., Smart Waste Bin IoT).
  4. Deliverable Cards:
    • Displays: Title, task ID code, COMPLETED green badge, project name, sprint name, points, and completion date.
    • Button 1: "Inspect Details" (Gray File Button):
      • Opens the Deliverable Inspection Modal.
      • Displays full requirements scope, original description, estimated vs. actual effort hours, and full discussion/comment log.
    • Button 2: "Reopen" (Amber Rotate Button):
      • If a task requires rework or QA found an issue, clicking Reopen moves it back to IN_PROGRESS.

6. Page 4: Drag-and-Drop Agile Kanban Board (/kanban)

Purpose: Visual workflow management representing sprint tasks moving through production stages.

The 6 Pipeline Columns

Column Stage Definition
BACKLOG Requirements gathered, waiting to be scheduled into a sprint.
TODO Committed to the current sprint; ready for an engineer to pick up.
IN_PROGRESS Active engineering, testing, or operational execution.
IN_REVIEW Pull Request open, QA verification, or manager review.
BLOCKED Work halted due to an external impediment or missing resource.
COMPLETED Done, tested, delivered, and accepted.

Interactive Actions

  • Drag-and-Drop: Grab any task card by holding the mouse and drop it into another column.
    • Automatically triggers PATCH /api/tasks/[id].
    • Saves the new status in SQLite/PostgreSQL with optimistic UI (updates instantly without flickering).
  • Filter Toolbar:
    • Project Filter: View Kanban for all projects or a single initiative.
    • Sprint Filter: Switch between active sprint and future sprints.
    • Assignee Filter: Filter cards assigned to a specific team member.
    • Priority Filter: View only URGENT or HIGH cards.

7. Page 5: Task Management Hub (/tasks)

Purpose: Enterprise-grade master table for querying, sorting, creating, and inspecting all organization tasks.

Controls & Buttons

  • Search Input: Live filter across task titles, descriptions, and IDs (T2T-1001).
  • Status & Priority Dropdowns: Narrow down tasks by execution state or urgency.
  • Button: "+ Create Task" (Super Admin & Managers):
    • Opens the Create Task Modal:
      • Title & Description: Detailed requirements or acceptance criteria.
      • Project & Sprint: Link task to an existing project and sprint.
      • Assignee: Assign to an employee or intern.
      • Priority: LOW, MEDIUM, HIGH, or URGENT.
      • Story Points: Select Fibonacci points (1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13).
      • Estimated Hours: Expected time commitment (e.g. 12 hrs).
    • Auto Task ID: Automatically generates the next sequential ID (e.g. T2T-1020).
  • Task Table Columns:
    • Task ID, Title, Project, Assignee Avatar, Priority Badge, Status Badge, Due Date, and Actions.
  • Task Detail & Discussion Modal (Clicking any Task):
    • View full task metadata.
    • Live Discussion Box: Write a comment or status update and click "Post Comment".
    • Comments are date-stamped and show the author's avatar.

14. Page 12: Compliance Audit Logs (/audit-logs)

Purpose: Immutable compliance ledger for tracking every critical action taken across the company.

Table Columns

  • Timestamp: Date and exact time of the action (in Asia/Kolkata time).
  • Actor / User: Full name, avatar, and role of the person who took the action.
  • Action Type:
    • USER_LOGIN: Session authenticated.
    • TASK_CREATED: New task added to system.
    • TASK_STATUS_CHANGED: Task moved across Kanban or completed.
    • BLOCKER_FLAGGED: Blocker raised.
    • PASSWORD_CHANGED: Security credential updated.
  • Object: Target project, task ID, or user modified.
  • Details: Specific payload changes (e.g. Changed status from TODO to IN_PROGRESS).
